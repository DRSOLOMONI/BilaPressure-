Safety comes first

This is an early prototype, not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you may be physically dependent on alcohol, do not suddenly stop without medical guidance: withdrawal can be dangerous. Contact a qualified healthcare professional. Seek urgent local help for severe symptoms or immediate danger.

If alcohol is affecting your safety or wellbeing, consider speaking with a qualified health professional or a trusted person who can help you find appropriate local support.

About Bila Pressure

A developing project exploring supportive habit reflection, responsible data insights, and—later—community resources for adults aged 18–29 in Kenya. The community and AI features are not active in this prototype.
