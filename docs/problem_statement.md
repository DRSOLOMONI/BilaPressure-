# Bila Pressure — Problem Statement

**Status:** Draft for discovery; assumptions are not findings.  
**Product promise:** *Your life. Your pace. Your choice.*

## Problem to investigate

Some adults who want to change their relationship with alcohol may find it difficult to reflect on their habits or follow through on personal intentions because of social pressure, stress, routines, or limited access to supportive, non-judgmental resources. Bila Pressure will test whether a private reflection tool, practical resources, and transparent progress insights are useful to the intended audience.

This is a hypothesis to validate—not a claim about all young adults in Kenya and not a claim that a digital product will reduce alcohol-related harm.

## Initial audience

Adults aged 18–29 in Kenya who are curious about their drinking habits or voluntarily want to make a change. Participation in research must be voluntary. The product must not target minors.

## Research questions

1. How do interested adults describe the situations in which alcohol-related choices feel difficult?
2. Which forms of support feel useful, respectful, culturally relevant, and safe?
3. Would people use a private check-in or personal-goal feature, and what would stop them?
4. What privacy concerns would prevent participation?
5. Which measures can be collected ethically and interpreted without overstating impact?

## Assumptions to test

- Some members of the intended audience want non-judgmental support.
- Social situations, stress, routines, or boredom may be relevant triggers for some participants.
- People may prefer private, low-friction tools over public disclosure.
- Clear privacy explanations may improve trust.
- The usefulness of a community feature is unknown and should be tested before development.

Do not treat these assumptions as facts until research supports them.

## MVP scope

### In the prototype
- Optional daily mood and intention check-in.
- Optional reflection on potential influences.
- Local-only storage in the current browser.
- A simple personal history and export/delete controls.
- General, non-diagnostic supportive reminders.
- Explicit safety and privacy notices.

### Not in the prototype
- No real AI model or AI-generated clinical advice.
- No accounts, cloud sync, server-side database, or remote analytics.
- No diagnosis, treatment, or claims of effectiveness.
- No public community, peer messaging, or moderation system.
- No collection of real participant health data for portfolio demonstrations.

## Measurement approach

Initially measure usability and voluntary engagement with the prototype, using synthetic test records and consented feedback where appropriate. Select any health-related outcome only after reviewing suitable evidence and consulting a qualified health professional. Define the metric, recall period, baseline, missing-data handling, and evaluation design before collecting or interpreting it. Self-reported outcomes have limitations and do not by themselves establish causality.

## Ethics, privacy, and safety

- Adults 18+ only.
- Use synthetic data for development and public demos.
- If conducting research, obtain informed consent, collect only necessary information, explain withdrawal and deletion, and seek appropriate ethical guidance.
- Do not commit personal, identifiable, or sensitive health information to GitHub.
- The browser prototype stores check-ins locally on the device; users sharing a device should be aware of that risk.
- Alcohol withdrawal can be dangerous. People who may be physically dependent should consult a qualified healthcare professional before stopping suddenly.
- Provide clear signposting to qualified support; urgent symptoms or immediate danger require urgent local assistance.

## Definition of a successful discovery phase

- Interview/research protocol reviewed for ethical and safety concerns.
- Feedback gathered voluntarily from an appropriate adult audience.
- Main needs, privacy concerns, and feature priorities documented without identifying participants.
- A go/no-go decision made for each proposed feature.
- No claims of effectiveness made from a prototype alone.
